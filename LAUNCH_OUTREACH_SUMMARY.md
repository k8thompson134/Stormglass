## Why This Matters

For people with chronic illnesses, weather often feels random but isn't. Pressure drops trigger migraines. Temperature swings worsen POTS. Humidity intensifies fibromyalgia flares. But without data, it's hard to prove the pattern—even to yourself.

Stormglass helps you:
- 📊 **See the correlation** between weather and your symptoms
- 🔍 **Discover YOUR specific triggers** (they might differ from research)
- 💬 **Talk to your doctor with data** instead of just "I think weather affects me"
- 🎯 **Predict flares** from 7-day weather forecasts
- 🛡️ **Prepare better** when conditions are likely to trigger symptoms

## Key Features

### Symptom Logging
- Rate severity (1-10)
- Tag which symptoms you had
- Add optional notes
- Automatic environmental snapshot captures weather at that moment

### Health Impact View
- See risk levels for the next 7 days
- Color-coded risk (green/yellow/orange/red) + icons (✓⚠⚠⚠)
- Understand which factors are spiking today
- Track how different conditions respond to different triggers

### 24-Hour Weather History
- Barometric pressure with chart
- Temperature, humidity, wind
- Air quality index
- Pollen/mold counts
- Geomagnetic activity (Kp index)

### Settings
- Select which conditions affect you
- Set your location (or change it anytime)
- Review your symptom logs
- Delete data you don't need

## Data Security & Privacy

### What We Store
- Your location (city/country) — only to get your weather
- Health conditions you selected
- Symptom logs with timestamps and severity
- Environmental snapshot when you logged (weather data at that time)

### What We DON'T Store
- Your name, email, or identity
- Browsing history or device tracking
- IP address or location tracking beyond what you set
- Payment info (it's free)
- Anything else

### How It's Protected
- 🔒 HTTPS encryption (data locked in transit)
- 🔐 Bearer token authentication (authorized access only)
- 🛡️ Rate limiting (prevents abuse)
- 🔄 No third-party tracking (no Google Analytics, no cookies)
- 📄 Full privacy policy (read it at PRIVACY_POLICY.md)

## Limitations to Know

### Not a Medical Device
- Can't diagnose ("you have migraines")
- Can't treat anything
- Can't replace your doctor
- Not FDA-approved or HIPAA-compliant

### Data Limitations
- Weather forecasts accurate 1-3 days out (less beyond that)
- Geomagnetic data lags 6-12 hours behind real-time
- Pollen data not available in all regions
- Microclimate variations not captured (mountains, valleys, coastal areas)

### What You Should Track Yourself
- Diet (caffeine, trigger foods)
- Sleep quality and hours
- Stress levels
- Medications/supplements
- Activity level and overexertion
- Menstrual cycle (if relevant)
- Alcohol consumption
- Environmental exposures (smoke, crowds, noise)

Use the "notes" field when logging symptoms to capture these factors.
